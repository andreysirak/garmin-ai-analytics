Open the generic Garmin chat UI:
  make chat-up starts the frontend port-forward automatically after deployment.
  Open the address on the 'Forwarding from' line (default port 5173).
  Use make chat-ui to reconnect later, or forward the Service directly:
  kubectl --context kind-garmin-ai-analytics -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ include "garmin-chat.name" . }}-frontend 5173:80
  http://127.0.0.1:5173

Model initialization status and logs:
  kubectl --context kind-garmin-ai-analytics -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get jobs -l app.kubernetes.io/component=model-pull
  kubectl --context kind-garmin-ai-analytics -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs job/{{ include "garmin-chat.name" . }}-model-pull-{{ .Release.Revision }}

The models PVC survives Pod replacement and Helm uninstall, but kind's default
node-local storage does not survive cluster deletion. See garmin-chat/README.md.
