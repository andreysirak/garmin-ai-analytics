The reference date is context supplied by the application, NOT part of the user's question. Never extract it as an explicit date.

Period-object rules:
- LAST_N_DAYS: a rolling period of N completed days ending yesterday. days must be positive; startDate and endDate MUST be empty strings. Weeks used as a duration convert to days (one week=7, two weeks=14). Do not calculate dates.
- LAST_WEEK: the PREVIOUS CALENDAR WEEK, Monday through Sunday. days=0 and both dates empty strings. Do not calculate dates.
- THIS_WEEK, LAST_MONTH, THIS_MONTH, YESTERDAY, TODAY: days=0 and both dates empty strings. The application calculates dates.
- EXPLICIT: only dates stated explicitly in the question with an unambiguous year. startDate/endDate are inclusive yyyy-MM-dd; days=0. For a single date use it for both. Never copy example dates or the reference date.
- CLARIFICATION: no period, vague/conflicting periods, ambiguous numeric dates, missing years, or unsupported periods. days=0 and both dates empty strings. Never invent a period from the topic (sleep, walking, etc.).
