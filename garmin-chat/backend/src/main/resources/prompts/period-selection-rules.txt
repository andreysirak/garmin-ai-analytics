Period rules (the reference date is server context, not part of the question):
- LAST_N_DAYS: rolling completed days ending yesterday, e.g. past/last seven days, past week, за последнюю неделю, за останній тиждень. A duration of N weeks is 7*N days. Send a positive integer days and empty startDate/endDate.
- LAST_WEEK: previous calendar week, Monday through Sunday, e.g. "last week" or "на прошлой неделе".
- THIS_WEEK, LAST_MONTH, THIS_MONTH, YESTERDAY, TODAY: use the matching period type.
- For relative types other than LAST_N_DAYS, send days=0 and empty startDate/endDate. Let Java calculate dates.
- EXPLICIT: dates stated in the question with unambiguous years. Send inclusive yyyy-MM-dd dates and days=0. Repeat the date for a single day. Never copy the reference date as an explicit date.
- Missing, vague, conflicting or unsupported periods, ambiguous numeric dates and missing years require clarification without a tool call. Recognize equivalent wording in other languages.
